Troubleshooting

Coming from v1.1.1? It was debug-signed, so Android will reject a release-signed APK over it. Uninstall v1.1.1 once before installing v1.3.7. Firebase data returns after sign-in; app-private files are cleared, so back up anything you need first. Later release-signed builds update in place.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Install, storage, offline reading, duplicate cleanup, and what the app does with your data.

How do I install and update?

Download the APK, allow install from this source, open the app, verify your email, and request access. v1.2.0 and later share the release key, so v1.3.7 updates in place; v1.1.1 still needs a one-time uninstall. From v1.3.2 onward, Settings → APP UPDATE can check for and install later releases.

Why do I need admin approval?

NIMELSSA Vault is meant for MLS students, but a school email is not required. After you verify any email address, submit your name, matric number, and level. A class rep in your level can vouch for you, and an admin gives the final approval. Staff accounts bypass the approval flag but still need a verified email.

How much space does it use?

The APK is about 4.7 MB. A fresh install takes roughly ~18 MB on disk before you save anything; that grows mostly with the course files you download. Files mirrored to a Download location also use space in that folder.

Will I lose my downloads or saved data?

If you are coming from v1.1.1, yes for local data: the one-time uninstall clears app-private files. Firebase-backed courses, resources, proposals, and your profile return after sign-in. Downloads made in v1.3.7 can be mirrored to a folder you choose and restored after a later reinstall.

What data is stored, and where?

Your profile, access request, approval status, and the shared course, resource, proposal, report, and notification data live in Firebase. Course files live in Google Drive. Offline copies and saved files stay on the device; if you choose a Download location, mirrored copies are also written to that folder under your control. The app requests internet, network state, and notification permissions, plus the folder you pick for downloads.

How does duplicate cleanup work?

Open the course as an admin. Cards that point to the same Drive file, or have identical MD5s on that shelf, show a warning and a “Delete this duplicate” button. Confirming removes the catalog card for everyone but leaves the Drive file untouched. The check is per shelf and only covers cards with a Drive file ID or MD5.

Can I use it offline?

Partially: Firestore keeps a disk cache, saved resources remain readable offline, and the course list falls back to the built-in seed catalog. Proposals, approvals, and notifications need a connection. Choose a Download location so saved files survive a reinstall.

What am I allowed to do with the app?

Nothing beyond personal use — NIMELSSA Vault is proprietary and all rights reserved. Its source is private, and the signed APK is offered on this site for personal use. No redistribution or publication without the author's permission. Share this site, not the APK.
